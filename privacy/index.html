01 Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to Inteople Limited ("Inteople", "we", "us"), a technology company based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and to the websites, applications and products we own and operate — including Healodex, EduNation, ReplyFlow AI and PalliShokti (together, our "Services").

Our role depends on the context. When you use a product we operate, or visit inteople.com, Inteople is the data controller. When we build or operate a platform on behalf of a client, that client is the controller and Inteople acts as a data processor — we process data only on their instructions, under a separate data processing agreement. This policy describes our practices as a controller; the client's own privacy notice governs data we process for them.

02 The data we collect

We collect only what a Service needs to function, and we're explicit about each category:

Information you provide

  • Contact & account data — name, email, phone, organisation and the credentials you create.
  • Communications — messages you send us through forms, email or support, and our replies.
  • Service content — data you enter into a product to use it (for example, records you create).

Information collected automatically

  • Usage & device data — pages viewed, features used, approximate location, IP address, browser and device type.
  • Cookies & similar technologies — as described in section 12.

Sensitive data

Some of our products handle sensitive categories — for example, health information in Healodex or data relating to children in EduNation. We treat these with heightened safeguards and only where there is a clear, lawful basis to do so. See section 6.

03 How we use your data

We use personal data to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain and secure our Services;
  • Authenticate users and protect against fraud and abuse;
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide support;
  • Understand how Services are used so we can improve them;
  • Send service-related communications, and — with your consent where required — relevant updates;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.
We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use the content you enter into a product to train third-party AI models.

04 Our legal basis for processing

Where data-protection law (such as the GDPR) applies, we rely on one or more of these bases:

  • Contract — to deliver a Service you've requested;
  • Legitimate interests — to secure, operate and improve our Services, balanced against your rights;
  • Consent — for optional cookies and certain communications, which you can withdraw at any time;
  • Legal obligation — where we're required by law to process or retain data.

05 Sharing & service providers

We share personal data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers (sub-processors) who help us run our Services — cloud hosting, infrastructure, analytics and communications — under contracts that require them to protect your data and use it only for us.
  • Clients, where we process data on their behalf as a processor.
  • Legal & safety — where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the public or Inteople.
  • Business transfers — if Inteople is involved in a merger, acquisition or asset sale, with notice to you.
Category Purpose
Cloud & hosting providers Running and storing our Services securely
Analytics providers Understanding aggregate usage to improve products
Communication tools Delivering email and support responses

A current list of sub-processors is available on request at privacy@inteople.com.

06 Product-specific notes

Healodex (health data)

Healodex processes health and clinical information. We apply HIPAA-style safeguards — encryption, strict access controls and audit logging — and process health data only to provide care services, on the instructions of the healthcare provider responsible for the patient relationship.

EduNation (students & children)

EduNation serves schools, which means we may process data about children at the school's direction. The school is the controller for student data; we act as processor, do not use student data for advertising, and apply data minimisation and access controls suited to an education setting.

ReplyFlow AI

ReplyFlow processes customer conversations to draft and route replies. Conversation content is used to provide the service to the operating business and is not used to train third-party models.

PalliShokti

PalliShokti handles financial and agricultural information for rural users. Financial data is protected with encryption and access controls appropriate to fintech, and used only to deliver the service.

07 How long we keep data

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected — to provide the Service, meet legal and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it. Specific retention periods vary by data type and product, and are governed by defined schedules rather than kept indefinitely by default.

08 How we protect data

We protect personal data with encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access controls, multi-factor authentication for our team, secure development practices, monitoring and audit logging, and tested backups. No system is perfectly secure, but security is something we engineer from the start rather than add at the end. You can read more on our Trust & Security page.

09 Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Delete your data, subject to legal exceptions;
  • Export your data in a portable format;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@inteople.com. We'll respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. If we process your data on behalf of a client, we'll direct your request to that client as the controller. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

10 International data transfers

Inteople operates from Bangladesh and uses cloud infrastructure that may store or process data in other countries. Where we transfer personal data across borders, we use appropriate safeguards — such as contractual protections — and, where you require it, can architect data residency to keep data within a specific region.

11 Children's privacy

Our public website and most Services are not directed at children. Where a Service does involve children — notably EduNation — we process that data only at the direction of the school or guardian responsible, with safeguards appropriate to a learning environment, and never for advertising.

12 Cookies & similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies to keep our sites working, remember your preferences, and understand aggregate usage so we can improve. Essential cookies are needed for the site to function; analytics cookies are optional. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and where required we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

13 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products and the law evolve. When we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Your continued use of our Services after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.
