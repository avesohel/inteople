The problem

Schools run on disconnected tools

Most schools in the region operate on paper registers, ad-hoc spreadsheets and personal messaging apps. Teachers lose hours to admin, leaders lack visibility, and parents are left guessing about attendance, results and fees — all in tools never designed to work together or in their language.

  • Manual admin drains teaching time
  • No real-time visibility for school leaders
  • Parents disconnected from their child's progress
  • Existing tools are costly, English-only, or both
Our solution

One platform for the whole school

EduNation unifies administration, teaching and family communication in a single bilingual system. An AI assistant helps teachers plan and grade, dashboards give leaders real visibility, and dedicated apps keep students and parents informed in Bangla or English.

  • Admissions, attendance, exams and fees in one place
  • AI assistant for lesson planning and faster feedback
  • Student & parent apps with real-time updates
  • Full Bangla + English across every role

Implementation

How we built it

PHASE 01 · Discovery

Workflow-first research

We mapped how schools, teachers and parents actually work — and the connectivity and device realities of the region — before designing a single screen.

PHASE 02 · Core platform

Multi-tenant foundation

We built tenant isolation and role-based access first, so a single school and a multi-branch group share one secure, scalable system.

PHASE 03 · Apps

Mobile-first, bilingual

Student, parent and teacher experiences were built mobile-first for entry-level devices, with Bangla + English throughout.

PHASE 04 · AI assist

Teacher-facing intelligence

We added an LLM assistant for planning, materials and feedback, scoped to support teachers and keep them in control.

PHASE 05 · Rollout

Onboarding & iteration

We refined onboarding so non-technical staff can adopt it quickly, and continue iterating from real classroom use.
