Product vision

Every school deserves the tools the best-resourced ones already have. EduNation exists to put a modern, intelligent operating system within reach of any school.

Schools run on a patchwork of registers, spreadsheets and messaging apps — work that drains teachers and leaves parents in the dark. EduNation replaces that with one connected system that handles administration, supports teaching with AI, and keeps families informed in the language they speak.

It's designed for emerging-market realities: affordable, mobile-first, bilingual, and usable on modest connectivity — so digital transformation isn't a privilege of elite institutions.

Admissions, attendance, timetables, exams, fees and reporting in one connected system.

AI classroom assistant

Student & parent apps

Family communication
