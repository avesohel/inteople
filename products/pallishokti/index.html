Product vision

The digital economy mostly skipped the village. PalliShokti exists to put the tools of the modern economy into rural hands — money, knowledge and markets.

Rural families face the same problems repeated across regions: limited access to formal finance, no reliable agricultural guidance, and middlemen standing between producers and fair prices. PalliShokti addresses all three in one trusted app — payments and savings, crop and weather intelligence, and direct market access.

It's engineered for how the countryside actually lives online: a low-bandwidth, bilingual, offline-friendly experience, with agent-assisted onboarding for those new to smartphones — so empowerment reaches the last mile, not just the city.
