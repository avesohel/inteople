Product vision

Quality healthcare shouldn't depend on being in the right place. Healodex exists to bring clinical-grade intelligence to wherever the patient is.

In much of the world, distance, connectivity and a shortage of specialists stand between people and timely care. Healodex closes that gap with a connected platform: affordable diagnostic devices capture vital readings, the system structures and analyzes them in real time, and doctors review and act remotely — even where the network is unreliable.

It's built as infrastructure, not a single app: an interoperable, offline-first foundation that health systems, clinics and practitioners can build care delivery on.

Core features

What Healodex does

IoT diagnostics

Connected devices capture vitals and readings that stream into the platform automatically — no manual transcription.

Doctor-in-the-loop triage

AI surfaces relevant signals and priorities; clinicians stay in control of every decision.

Offline-first sync

Capture and work without a connection; data reconciles safely when the network returns.

Unified patient records

Readings, history and consultations in one structured, interoperable record.

Remote consultations

Connect patients and doctors for teleconsultation with the clinical context already loaded.

Compliance-aware by design

HIPAA-aware data handling, access control and audit trails built into the foundation.

Technical highlights

The engineering underneath

Edge + IoT integration

Device firmware and edge processing feed readings into the platform with secure device identity.

Offline-first data layer

Local-first storage with conflict-aware sync keeps care moving through connectivity gaps.

Applied AI triage

Models surface signals and prioritize cases, always under clinician review — never autonomous diagnosis.

FHIR-ready & HIPAA-aware
